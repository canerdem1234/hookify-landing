FAQ

What is LinkedIn's character limit for posts?

LinkedIn allows up to 3,000 characters per post. If you go over, the post won't publish until you cut it down.

Where does LinkedIn's "See more" cutoff happen?

On desktop, LinkedIn truncates posts and shows "See more" after roughly 210 characters. On mobile, the cutoff is shorter, typically around 140 characters. The exact number can vary slightly by LinkedIn's current layout.

Why does the "See more" cutoff matter?

Everything before the cutoff is what people see in their feed before deciding whether to click "See more" and read the rest. A weak first line means most of your audience never reads past it.
